A portfolio should tell a recruiter how the codebase will behave after the interview. I want it to communicate that the work is organized, recoverable, and easy to support when the product changes shape.

That means designing around explicit ownership, smaller interfaces, and well-named layers. It also means avoiding cleverness that makes a future handoff expensive.

The goal is not minimal code at any cost. The goal is code that keeps its intent under pressure so a team can extend it without losing confidence.
