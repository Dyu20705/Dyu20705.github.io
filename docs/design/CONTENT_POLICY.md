# Content ownership

Personal copy must be supported by owner instructions or owner-provided evidence.
No generated motivation, personal history, employment, novelty or performance
claims. Missing personal writing stays absent. Blog posts require explicit
`ownerWritten: true` and `draft: false`; unapproved posts must not reach routes,
lists, RSS or the sitemap. Historical generated posts are removed, not replaced.

Featured claims are checked against current default-branch source, documentation,
release metadata and workflow runs. A plan is never an implemented capability.
Project pages carry evidence links and the verification date. Recheck when updating.
Keep uncertain skills as development areas, not proficiency scores.

OWNER MUST WRITE: future engineering/research articles and any personal narrative
beyond the current factual introduction. The existing PDF is owner-provided and
unchanged; the owner must update it separately if its dates or identity change.
